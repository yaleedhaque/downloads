Bluetooth Remote HID

Your phone becomes a wireless keyboard, touchpad, air-mouse & media remote.

Platforms: Android · Version 1.2.6

What it is

Bluetooth Remote HID turns your Android phone into a fully wireless input device for any PC over Bluetooth Classic + LE HID. No host software to install, no cloud — your phone just pairs and works.

Features

  • Wireless keyboard with media keys
  • Touchpad with multi-touch gestures
  • Air-mouse motion control
  • Media remote (play / pause / next / volume)
  • No host software — standard Bluetooth HID pairing

How to use

  1. Download and install the APK on your Android phone.
  2. Open Settings > Bluetooth on your PC and make it discoverable.
  3. In the app, tap Pair and select your PC.
  4. Choose your input surface: keyboard, touchpad, air-mouse or media remote.
  5. Start controlling your PC — no host software needed.
