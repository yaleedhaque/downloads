How this works & why the source is private

The finished products you can see and use — the websites, the apps, the downloads — are published here for everyone, free.
The source code, configuration, extensions and internals behind them live in private repositories and are not distributed. This page only ever hosts built outputs (APKs, executables, scripts, standalone files). Want something added or changed? Open an issue.
