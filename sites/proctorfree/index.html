Everything You Need
Enterprise-grade proctoring features, all running locally in your browser.
📷

Face Detection & Liveness

TensorFlow.js detects your face and verifies you're a real person — not a photo or deepfake. Runs entirely in your browser.

👁️

Gaze & Head Tracking

Monitors eye movement and head position. Flags when you look away from the screen for extended periods.

🔒

Browser Lockdown

Detects tab switches, copy/paste attempts, right-clicks, and window unfocus. All events logged with timestamps.

🎥

Screen & Webcam Recording

Records your screen and webcam simultaneously. Saved locally by default — you control your data.

📊

Session Reports

Generates a complete integrity report with event counts, timestamps, and a suspicious activity score.

🛡️

Teacher Console

Teachers toggle exactly which anti-cheating safeguards run per exam — face, recording, lockdown, environment — and save their own question paper.

🆓

Zero Cost, Zero Tracking

No accounts, no cookies, no analytics, no data sent anywhere. Open source and auditable.

How It Works
Three simple steps. No installation needed.
1

Set Up Your Exam

Enter your name, exam title, and duration. No account needed — teachers can pre-tune safeguards in the Teacher Console.

2

Allow Camera Access

Grant webcam permission. AI loads directly in your browser — nothing leaves your machine.

3

Take & Submit

Complete your exam. Get a detailed integrity report when done.

☁️ Cloud Recording Storage

By default, all recordings stay on your device (private & free forever). Toggle this ON to attempt uploading recordings to cloud storage for later review by proctors. This requires server storage that costs money.

🛡️ Teacher Console

One anti-cheating policy per test. Toggle exactly which safeguards run during the exam — the student's copy silently honors this config, which is saved on this device and applied to every exam launched from here.

✍️ Question Paper

One question per line: Question ? A. option | B. option | C. option | D. option %correct-letter. Example: What is 2+2 ? A. 3 | B. 4 | C. 5 | D. 6 %B

Honest by design: every safeguard runs entirely in this browser and is a demonstration, not a certified anti-cheat. No data from this console ever leaves this device.
